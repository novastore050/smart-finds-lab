# Reference: freakin-reviews

1. Fill `reference.yaml` (titles, dates, lengths, views, descriptions).
2. Optional: save transcripts as `transcripts/<video_id>.txt` (copied from YouTube's *Show transcript*). They stay on your computer (not uploaded to GitHub) and are used to analyse hooks/pacing and to make sure our scripts never copy their wording.
3. Optional: `--video-file` with a local copy you are allowed to analyse, to measure cuts per minute.
4. Run: `python scripts/studio.py reference-analyze freakin-reviews`

Use references for inspiration only: never reuse their script, footage, thumbnails or music.
