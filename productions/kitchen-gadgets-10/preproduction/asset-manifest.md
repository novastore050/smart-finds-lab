## 2. Product images (one hero photo per product + the detail crops the plan uses)

**Rule:** do **not** use Amazon listing images. Amazon's Associates/Product Advertising API terms only allow product images to be shown through Amazon's own API/links (no downloading or caching), and the images belong to the brand or seller. Retailer images (Target, Walmart, etc.) are equally not licensed to us.

Legitimate sources, in order of preference:

1. **Your own photos** of a unit you own (you own the copyright; showing the product to review it is normal practice). Needs the product in hand - buying is your decision; nothing has been purchased.
2. **Manufacturer press / media images with written permission** (email the brand's press or customer contact; keep the reply). Usage for a monetised YouTube review must be stated in the request.
3. Openly licensed images (e.g. Wikimedia Commons, CC BY/CC0) - none were found for these exact products; check before relying on this.
