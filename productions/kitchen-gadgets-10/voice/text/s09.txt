Number four: the ox-oh Good Grips salad spinner. Wet lettuce is the reason so many salads end up soggy, and patting leaves dry with paper towels takes forever. Here you press the pump on top with one hand, the basket spins the water off, and a brake button stops it when you're done. The basket doubles as a colander, a non-skid base keeps it steady, and the parts are dishwasher safe. Reviewers like how easy it is to use and how much it holds. The honest downside is size: it's about ten inches across, so it takes real cabinet space, and some reviewers find it less sturdy than pricier spinners.
