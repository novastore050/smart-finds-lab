Number seven is the Micro-plane Premium Classic zester. Zest a lemon on a box grater, and the bitter white pith tends to come along for the ride. This long, narrow grater has sharp, photo-etched stainless steel teeth that take just the zest, and they turn hard cheese like parmesan into light, fluffy shreds. Ginger, garlic and nutmeg work too, and Micro-plane says it comes with a reusable protective cover. Buyers mostly praise how sharp it is and how easy it is to clean. The trade-offs: it costs more than a basic grater, and that blade demands care at the sink.
