Number seven: the ox-oh Good Grips soap-dispensing dish brush. Sponges get smelly fast, and scrubbing a greasy pan usually means your hands sit in hot, soapy water. This brush keeps dish soap in the handle. Press the button and it flows straight to the bristles. There's a scraper on the back for baked-on bits, and the bristles are safe for nonstick pans. Apartment Therapy's editors say it makes doing the dishes easier. The most common complaint is that the soap can leak or come out unevenly, and replacement heads are an ongoing cost. For everyday dishes, though, it's a simple upgrade. Now, something for your mornings.
