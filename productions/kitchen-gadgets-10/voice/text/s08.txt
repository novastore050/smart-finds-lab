Number five: the Power Licks handheld milk frother. Milk warmed in the microwave just sits there, flat. This little battery-powered whisk whips it into foam in under a minute, and it comes with a stand. Use it for lattes, matcha or hot chocolate. Review roundups call it easy to use and durable. Two drawbacks: it's loud, and it needs two double-A batteries that aren't included. Next, the job that makes a lot of us cry: chopping onions.
