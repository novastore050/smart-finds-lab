Number ten: the Zoo-lay two-in-one lemon squeezer. Squeezing citrus by hand is messy. Seeds end up in your food, and a lot of juice stays in the fruit. This hinged metal press has two bowl sizes, one for lemons and one for limes, and the seeds stay behind in the bowl. Buyers often mention how sturdy it feels and how easily it rinses clean. The catch? It isn't built for big oranges, small seed bits can slip through with key limes, and one buyer reported the paint chipping after a few months. If you cook with lemon or make drinks at home, it earns its drawer space. And speaking of jobs that take longer than they should...
