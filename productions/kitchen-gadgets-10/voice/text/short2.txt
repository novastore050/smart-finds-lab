If your lemon zest tastes bitter, your grater might be the problem. A box grater digs into the white pith under the peel, and that's where the bitterness lives. The Micro-plane Premium Classic has a long, narrow blade with sharp, photo-etched teeth that shave off just the bright outer zest. It also turns parmesan into fluffy shreds and handles ginger and garlic. The honest catch: that blade needs care when you wash it, so use the protective cover Micro-plane says comes with it. It also costs more than a basic grater. We didn't test it ourselves; this is from Micro-plane's specs and buyer reviews. What would you zest first?
