Number three: the ox-oh Good Grips salad spinner, because wet lettuce makes for a soggy salad. Instead of patting leaves dry with paper towels, you press the pump on top with one hand, the basket spins the water off, and a brake button stops it. According to the listing, the basket doubles as a colander, the parts are dishwasher safe, and a non-skid base keeps it steady. Reviewers like how easy it is and how much it holds. The trade-off is size: it's big and takes up real cabinet space, and some reviewers find it less sturdy than pricier spinners. Now, from taste to food safety.
