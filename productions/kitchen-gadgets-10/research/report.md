## Method

Candidates chosen for everyday usefulness, price accessibility and strong buyer/reviewer evidence. Ratings, benefits and drawbacks were collected from editorial reviews, retailer pages and review aggregators. Each value cites its source; quality of each source is labelled.

- amazon.com could not be opened from the research environment: NO value is verified against the live Amazon page.
- 'Amazon' prices/ratings shown are either search-engine snapshots (capture date unknown) or figures reported by third parties.
- Several sources are affiliate roundups with generic text; editorial hands-on reviews are noted where available.
- Some figures appeared in search summaries without a clearly attributable page - marked 'attribution unconfirmed'.
- Products were not purchased or tested. The video must stay research-based (no testing claims).
