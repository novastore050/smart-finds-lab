## Method

Exact listings were re-checked by searching each ASIN on amazon.com through a search engine (title, brand, model number and any rating shown on the Amazon page). Benefits and drawbacks come from editorial reviews, retailer pages and Amazon review summaries, each with its source.

## How the ranking works

Each shortlisted product gets 1-5 points for: Evidence (Amazon rating volume + independent reviews), Visual demo (how clearly the benefit shows on camera), Everyday benefit (how often a typical household would use it) and Clear problem/solution. Highest total = #1. Ties go to the stronger evidence. Scores are editorial judgements based on the sourced data, not measurements.

## Limitations

- amazon.com could not be opened from the research environment: NO value is verified against the live page.
- Amazon values come from search-engine snapshots of Amazon pages; capture dates are unknown and may be stale.
- Some Amazon ratings were shown on a sibling listing or review page of the same product - noted per field.
- Several third-party sources are affiliate roundups; editorial hands-on reviews are labelled.
- Products were not purchased or tested. The video stays research-based (no testing claims).
