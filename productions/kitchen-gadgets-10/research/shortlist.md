### Why each product (countdown order)

- **#10 Zulay Kitchen Metal 2-in-1 Lemon Lime Squeezer** — Cheap, satisfying visual and solid Amazon evidence (≈37,677 ratings, 4.7★), but the narrowest everyday use and the most overlap with other prep tools - so it opens the countdown at #10.
  - *Check on Amazon:* the rating count on Amazon (37,677) differs from a third-party figure (47,137) - record the live one
- **#9 KitchenAid All Purpose Kitchen Shears with Protective Sheath, 8.72 Inch, Black** — KEPT: the most versatile everyday tool in the list (herbs, pizza, packaging, chicken), the model is resolved (KE351OHOBA), and the same ASIN shows 4.7-4.8★ from 63-65K ratings on Amazon Australia/Singapore plus '20K+ bought in past month' on Amazon.com. Ranked #9 because the visual is less dramatic and it is a prep tool.
  - *Check on Amazon:* confirm the listing shows model KE351OHOBA; record the Amazon.com rating (only Australia/Singapore figures found for this ASIN)
- **#8 Microplane Premium Classic Series Zester/Grater, Black** — Highest Amazon star rating found (4.8★, 47,796 ratings) and a lovely visual, but a narrower everyday use.
  - *Check on Amazon:* each colour has its own model number - 46020 is black
- **#7 OXO Good Grips Soap Dispensing Dish Brush with Handle** — KEPT (owner choice), ASIN corrected to the current listing B0BK2R8TQB because the old one is unavailable. Only cleaning product; useful every day. Evidence is weaker than thought: no Amazon.com rating found (Singapore 4.5★/30,200, Canada 4.3★/3,975).
  - *Check on Amazon:* confirm B0BK2R8TQB is the single brush (not a 2-pack or the palm brush); record the Amazon.com rating (only Singapore/Canada figures found)
- **#6 PowerLix Milk Frother Handheld with Stainless Steel Stand - Deep Blue** — Adds coffee variety and a great foam visual (4.5★, 66,496 ratings on the Deep Blue review page), but editorial testing of this brand is thin.
  - *Check on Amazon:* confirm B08CVPQJHJ opens the Deep Blue colour with the stand; record the rating shown on that page
- **#5 Fullstar Pro Original Vegetable Chopper & Spiralizer (4-in-1)** — Best before/after demo and strong Amazon volume (4.6★, 67,144 ratings on its review page), but it is one of five prep tools, so lower on variety. Honest con: cleaning.
  - *Check on Amazon:* ASIN changed to B0764HS4SL (the listing showing model TF1096C-8); confirm colour/blade count; Fullstar sells many similar versions (2-in-1 to 14-in-1) - make sure the link is the 4-in-1
- **#4 OXO Good Grips Salad Spinner, 6.22 Qt** — KEPT: the product identity is clear (ASIN B00004OCKR titled 'OXO Good Grips 6.22 Qt Salad Spinner', #1 Best Seller in Salad Spinners), it is the most consistently recommended product in editorial roundups, and it has the strongest wet-vs-dry visual. Its Amazon.com rating is still missing (same-ASIN non-US figures: 4.5-4.7★, ~37K).
  - *Check on Amazon:* record the Amazon.com star rating and count for B00004OCKR (only non-US Amazon figures found); confirm size (6.22 Qt) and model on the listing
- **#3 TempPro TempSwift TP19H Instant Read Meat Thermometer** — The most useful item for food safety and unique in the list. Below the top two because accuracy/durability feedback is mixed and the advertised reading time was overstated (manual: 3-4 seconds).
  - *Check on Amazon:* brand shows as TempPro (ThermoPro rebrand); manufacturer page calls it 'TempSwift PLUS TP19H'; check whether the 'newer version' should be featured instead
- **#2 Rubbermaid Brilliance Leak-Proof Food Storage Containers with Airtight Lids, Set of 3 (6 Pieces Total)** — NEW (replaces the OXO Garlic Press, different brand). Adds a storage category, a strong 'flip it upside down' leak demo, broad everyday usefulness, and a test-kitchen win for the product line.
  - *Check on Amazon:* record the live rating count (Amazon.com page showed 1,900 vs 83,371 on Amazon Singapore); confirm it is the 3-container / 6-piece set
- **#1 Dash Rapid Egg Cooker, 6 Egg Capacity, Black (DEC005BK)** — Highest total: the strongest Amazon evidence (about 112,000 ratings, 4.7★ - a rounded snapshot figure) plus a hands-on editorial verdict, the only small appliance, and a very clear steam-and-eggs visual.
