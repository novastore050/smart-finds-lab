Reviewed: 2026-11-03 · Reviewed version: approved `script/script.yaml` (commit 661ce9c) — **left unchanged**.

## 1. Verdict

The approved script is **accurate, honest and publishable**, but it is **too uniform for 8.6 minutes**: every product
runs 42–52 s in the same order (name → problem → features → praise → downsides → bridge). The proposal keeps every
fact and caveat, but tightens it to **~7.4 min**, varies the openings, adds a mid-video re-hook, pays off the hook at #1,
and removes four wording risks.

| Criterion | Approved script | Main issue | Proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Natural American English | Good | Some long, list-like sentences (avg 18 words in #9, #3, #1; longest 33) | Shorter sentences, more spoken rhythm |
| Opening hook | OK | 14 s (target ≤10 s); the promise lands late | 9–10 s, promise in the second sentence |
| Retention | Weak middle | 15 s "quick note" right after the hook is a classic drop-off point; no re-hook; 10 near-identical segments | Disclosure cut to ~11 s; halfway re-hook after #6; hook paid off at #1; segments 36–47 s, growing toward #1 |
| Transitions | Good | 2 weak bridges ("speaking of jobs that take longer…", "that brings us to number one") | Concrete bridges (knife work → shears; sink → dish brush; lunch bag → containers) |
| Repetition | Medium | "buyers" ×6, "reviewers" ×5, "drawer" ×3, "a lot" ×4, "though" ×4 | buyers ×4, reviewers ×4, drawer ×1, a lot ×2, though ×2 |
| Accuracy | Good | 4 wording risks (see §3) | All four fixed or attributed |
| Honest pros/cons | Very good | One con generalised from a single review | Attributed to "one reviewer" |
| Sourced vs unverified | Good in files, invisible to viewers where it matters | ATK claim (#2) not flagged; frother speed unattributed | Flagged / attributed in narration |
