## 2. Checklist

| Check | Result | Notes / fixes made |
|---|---|---|
| Natural American English | ✅ | Average sentence length is 14.5 words. Sentences over 30 words were split; the longest left (29 words) are lists. Contractions and conversational bridges throughout. |
| Retention and pacing | ✅ | Hook ~13 s with the #1 payoff promised. Disclosure ~16 s. Product scenes 30–49 s, building toward #2 and #1. Halfway re-hook moved to the end of #6, with a teaser for #5. #1 opens by paying off the hook. Estimated total **7.6 min** + intro/outro. |
| Repetitive language | ✅ | "buyers" 6→4, "drawer" 3→2, "next" 5→3, "the catch" 2, "trade-off(s)" 2. Each scene's downside line now opens differently ("The catch", "On the downside", "The trade-offs", "Two drawbacks", "It's not perfect"). |
| Product-specific evidence | ✅ (with stated limits) | Evidence that covered a different version was removed earlier (Dash Deluxe, ATK 10-piece, Fullstar 4-blade lab test, older-version brush review). Where reviews don't name the model (Fullstar, brush leak reports, KitchenAid shears), the narration says so or attributes the claim to the product line. |
| Clear attribution | ✅ | Every spec is tied to its source: "Microplane says", "OXO says", "according to its manual", "according to the listing", "The Grilling Dad's review", "customer reviews", "Rubbermaid's promise". Full table: `fact-check.md`. |
| No implied personal testing | ✅ | The automated check found no testing phrases. "We didn't test…" appears in the long video and in every Short. No first-person use or results. |
| No unsupported superlatives | ✅ | Removed or reframed: "very sharp" → "sharp, photo-etched" (listing), "feel ancient" → "feel clumsy", "the one most likely to change your mornings" → "our pick for…". No "best", "#1 rated", "top-rated" or "game-changer". |
| No unsupported sales/popularity claims | ✅ | No sales ranks, "bestseller", rating counts, "millions" or "everyone's buying". "Buyers often mention…" describes review content, not popularity. |
