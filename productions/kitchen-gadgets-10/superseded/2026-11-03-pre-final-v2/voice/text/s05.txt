Number eight: the Micro-plane Premium Classic zester. A box grater is clumsy for small jobs, and zesting a lemon usually drags the bitter white pith along with it. This long, narrow grater has very sharp stainless steel teeth that take just the zest, and they turn hard cheese like parmesan into light, fluffy shreds. It also handles ginger, garlic and nutmeg, and it comes with a protective cover. Buyers mostly praise how sharp it is and how easy it is to clean. The trade-offs: it costs more than a basic grater, and that blade deserves respect when you wash it. Of course, once you've cooked, there's the cleanup.
