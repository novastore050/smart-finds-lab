# Content rules: honesty, copyright, affiliate links

These protect the channel. They are guidance, not legal advice, so check the current YouTube,
Amazon Associates and FTC/ASA rules for your country.

## 1. Honesty: research, not testing
Smart Finds Lab **researches and compares** products (specifications, verified customer reviews,
comparisons). Unless you really used a product:

- ✅ "Based on thousands of reviews…", "Buyers say…", "On paper, it…", "The main complaint is…"
- ❌ "I tested…", "After using it for a month…", "In my tests…", "I bought…"

`check` and the QC report **warn** when narration or on-screen text contains testing claims.
Also: don't show a product doing something it can't do (watch out for AI-generated footage).

## 2. Music, footage and sound
- Use only music you have a licence for: YouTube Studio → Audio Library is free and safe. Record the
  source in `music_license:` (QC reminds you).
- The sound effects in `assets/sound-effects/generated/` are synthesised by this system, so they are
  original and free to use.
- Fonts (Inter) are under the SIL Open Font License, so they are free for commercial video.
- Only use footage you created, generated (check your AI tool's commercial terms), or licensed.
  Manufacturer/Amazon listing videos and other creators' videos are **not** free to reuse.
- Product photos: your own, or press images whose terms allow review use.

## 3. Amazon affiliate links
- Put a clear disclosure in the description, e.g. *"As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying
  purchases."* A short spoken or on-screen mention near the start is good practice (FTC / ASA).
- Don't show exact prices burned into the video: they go out of date and Amazon's programme
  policies restrict how prices are shown. Say "check the link for today's price" instead.
  (The default outro note already says this.)
- Tick **"Includes paid promotion"** in YouTube Studio if a brand paid you or gave you the product.

## 4. AI disclosure
If you use realistic AI-generated scenes, tick YouTube's **altered or synthetic content** setting
when it applies.
